# Privacy Policy — Last Pride Standing : Tower Defence

**Effective date:** October 5, 2026

> **Summary:** Last Pride Standing : Tower Defence is an offline game. Any player account you create in the game exists only on your device. The game does not collect, sell, or transmit personal information to the developer or to anyone else.

## 1. About this privacy policy

This privacy policy explains how information is handled when you use **Last Pride Standing : Tower Defence** (the "Game"), published by **rakkarajput** ("we", "us", or "our").

## 2. Information we collect

The Game is designed to operate offline and does not ask you to provide personal information to us.

To keep separate progress for different players, the Game lets you create a local player account with a username and a password. The username can be any nickname. The password is never stored as typed: only a salted, one-way hash of it is kept. The account exists only on your device and is never sent to the developer or any server.

The Game does not collect or transmit personal information such as your name, email address, postal address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or camera data.

## 3. Game data stored on your device

The Game stores gameplay information locally on your device, such as local player accounts (username and password hash), game progress, levels cleared, stars, scores, the local Hall of Fame, settings and preferences. This data is encrypted on the device.

This locally stored game data is used only to provide gameplay features and is not transmitted to the developer by the Game. Uninstalling the Game or deleting its save folder removes it.

## 4. Internet and network access

The Game is designed for offline play. It does not require an internet connection.

If a future version adds online services, analytics, advertising, cloud synchronization, multiplayer, or other network functionality, this privacy policy will be updated to describe the applicable information practices before or when those features are introduced.

## 5. Advertising and analytics

The current version of the Game does not include third-party advertising or analytics services.

If third-party advertising, analytics, telemetry, or similar services are added in a future version, this policy will be updated to identify the relevant services and explain the information they process.

## 6. Purchases and app stores

The Game may be distributed through the Microsoft Store and other app stores. Those stores may process information associated with your account, purchases, downloads, device, or store activity under their own terms and privacy policies. The developer does not receive or control that information. For Microsoft's practices, see [Microsoft Privacy](https://privacy.microsoft.com/).

## 7. Children's privacy

The Game does not collect personal information from children or require children to provide personal information to play.

If you believe personal information has been provided to us through a method not covered by this policy, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

## 8. Data security

Because the Game does not transmit personal information to the developer, there is no developer-operated online database containing player information. Information stored locally by the Game is encrypted and remains subject to the security of the device on which the Game is installed.

## 9. Third-party services

The Game does not use third-party services to collect personal information. The app store and the device's operating system are separate services and may process information under their own policies.

## 10. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this privacy policy when the Game, its features, or its information practices change. The updated policy will be published at this same location with a revised effective date.
